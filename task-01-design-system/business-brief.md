# Business Brief: Liz's Grillz Bookings

## The business
Liz's Grillz is a Lagos caterer serving individuals, churches and offices; churches and offices bring the most revenue. The app complements WhatsApp: Liz sends a link, and the client checks a date, books and pays online.

## The person who uses it
The event organiser, aged 25 to 60: a church admin, office manager or individual planning a celebration. Tech confidence varies. They use phones, often on mobile data, so performance is a real constraint. Today, bookings and transfers happen over WhatsApp, so details and payment proof get lost in long threads.

## The one thing that must work
A client books a date, pays a deposit, and pays the balance five days before the event. Each payment shows as paid only after the payment gateway confirms it, and every confirmed payment is saved.

## Three questions only we can answer
1. "How much for X guests?" Answered from a fixed price list.
2. "Are you available on this date?" Answered from real kitchen capacity.
3. "How much is logistics, and can we get a discount?" Logistics is priced separately, and discounts grow with the amount spent.

## Money rules
- Deposit: 80% for individuals, 60% for churches and offices, who pay more slowly because of committee or finance approval.
- Balance is due five days before the event.
- A date is held for 48 hours awaiting the deposit, then released.
- Headcount can be reduced up to seven days before the event, and the balance is recalculated.
- Deposits are refundable only if the client cancels more than 14 days out.
- Offices receive an invoice and receipt for every payment.

## Admin
Liz manages prices, capacity and discounts, and handles exceptions such as late payments.

## Success looks like
Fewer WhatsApp messages per booking, and deposits confirmed within a day.

## Limits
Growth is capped by kitchen capacity, not software, so the app's value is faster conversion and steadier cash flow. We hold names, phone numbers and addresses, so Nigerian data protection law (NDPA) applies.

## How it should feel
Like a well-run family business, not a corporate firm: warm, clear, trusted and experienced. Prices are visible, the layout is clean, pages are fast, and every payment step is clearly confirmed.
