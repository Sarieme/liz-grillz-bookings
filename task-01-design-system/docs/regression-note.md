When I changed the brand colour in one place, the heading, the “Check a date” button and the price all turned purple, but the tagline stayed orange because I had pointed it at a raw hex value instead of the palette, so the page now shows two competing identities. The purple also changed the feel of the product: orange reads as festive and appetising, like a restaurant, while purple reads as a cold, corporate service, the opposite of the warm, trusted tone in my brief. This is the whole argument for token referencing: one edit at the source can rebrand the whole app, but a single hardcoded value silently opts out of it, and on a real app with hundreds of screens that mismatch would be hard to find and would make the product look unfinished.
