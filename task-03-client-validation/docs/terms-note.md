# Terms and Conditions Provenance Note

## 1. Provenance of Legal Text: The Honest Answer
The Terms of Service and Privacy Policy consent language implemented in Liz's Grillz Bookings was adapted from standard commercial catering service agreements and vetted Nigerian Data Protection Act (NDPA) regulatory compliance templates used by hospitality and event vendors in Lagos.

**What is not honest:**
- Claiming that an engineer wrote legally binding terms from scratch without legal reference.
- Claiming that an AI agent generated valid legal terms and that this is sufficient for production commerce.

Both claims represent dangerous professional failures. Commercial contracts involving financial transactions, non-refundable deposits, and personal identifying data (names, phone numbers, and event addresses) create binding liabilities under Nigerian law. In a production deployment, these terms must be formally reviewed and ratified by a qualified Nigerian legal practitioner.

## 2. Core Business Clauses Enforced by These Terms
The terms reflect the six commercial rules specified in the Liz's Grillz business brief:

1. **Date Hold Expiration:** A requested event date is reserved temporarily for exactly 48 hours awaiting deposit payment. If no payment confirmation is received within 48 hours, the date is automatically released back to kitchen capacity.
2. **Deposit Split by Client Category:**
   - Individuals: 80% upfront deposit upon booking.
   - Churches, NGOs, and Corporate Offices: 60% upfront deposit, accommodating organizational procurement cycles.
3. **Balance Settlement:** The outstanding balance is strictly due five days before the event date.
4. **Headcount Adjustment Window:** Event organizers may adjust their guest headcount upward or downward up to seven days prior to the event, triggering an automatic recalculation of the final balance.
5. **Cancellation & Refund Policy:** Deposits are non-refundable if cancellation occurs within 14 days of the scheduled event date, because Liz commits capital to purchase perishable proteins and ingredients in advance.
6. **Data Privacy (NDPA Compliance):** Client contact details are collected strictly for catering delivery, invoicing, and logistics. Data is encrypted at rest and never shared with third-party advertisers.
